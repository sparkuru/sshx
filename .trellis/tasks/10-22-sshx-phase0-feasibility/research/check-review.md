## Transfer selection constraint

The tested SFTP-v3 exclusive leaf create and baseline no-overwrite rename establish leaf collision behavior on OpenSSH 9.2; they do not bind a pathname's parent/root identity atomically. The raw library calls accept filename strings, not an `openat`-style directory capability. A private 0700 operation directory owned by the SSH account cannot exclude another process with that same remote UID from renaming/replacing it. A pre/post `lstat` may detect a tested substitution but leaves an inter-RPC TOCTOU interval and is not proof of race-free scope enforcement. A controlled cooperative fixture can demonstrate the mechanics; mutable parent/root guarantees remain a precise no-go for general MVP promotion. Unsupported binding must fail closed in the product rather than be advertised as a universally viable B backend. This architectural constraint is reported to main; the reviewer does not introduce a remote Agent or another full backend to conceal it.
